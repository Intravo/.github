## About us

Intravo Corp builds the software behind interactive meetings and conferences. Organizers use it to engage, translate, and measure every moment of an event, whether on-site, virtual, or hybrid.

Since 2017 our team has delivered events in 38+ countries and 60+ languages. We design, build, and run the whole stack ourselves, so you get one ecosystem instead of patchwork.

**One ecosystem · Clear pricing · Your data secured**

## The platform

| | |
|---|---|
| **Jybe mobile app** | Agendas, networking, live polling, gamification, and a social feed, fully branded for each event. |
| **Second Screen** | Tablets become an interactive channel for notes, saved slides, and real-time feedback. |
| **Interpretation** | Live human and AI interpretation that puts every attendee in their own language. |
| **Registration** | Custom forms, QR check-in, kiosk mode, and secure payments. |
| **Webcast** | HD live streaming with about one second of latency and built-in interactivity. |
| **Analytics** | Post-event reporting that turns attendance and engagement into insight. |
| **Custom & white-label** | Bespoke platforms and full production services, built around your event and your brand. |
